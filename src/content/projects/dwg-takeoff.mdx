Before a construction job can be priced, someone measures its drawings: square
metres of paving, metres of curb, how many trees. DWG Takeoff takes that request
in plain words and returns quantities you can check entity by entity. Every
number comes with its formula, the AutoCAD handles of what was measured, what
was counted once instead of twice and why, and the geometry highlighted on the
plan.

The AI never produces a number. A deterministic step first breaks the drawing
into a numbered catalogue of everything measurable. The model only picks the
entries that answer the request and the factors that apply, a geometry engine
does the measuring, and the model reviews the result before it's shown. On the
demo drawing, all eight quantities come back exact, in about 40 seconds.
