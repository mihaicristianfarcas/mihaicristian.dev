Traffic recorded today can be decrypted in ten years, once quantum computers
catch up. My BSc thesis closes that window for WireGuard. It adds ML-KEM-768,
the FIPS 203 standard, alongside the X25519 handshake already there, so an
attacker has to break both, and switching early can't cost you the security
you had.

It costs about 97 µs per handshake on an Apple M4 and 331 µs on a Raspberry
Pi 5. Both messages still fit in a single packet, and throughput doesn't
change.
