Other wallpaper apps hand you a gallery and hope you like something in it.
Supernova runs whatever you can render: a video, a Shadertoy shader compiled to
Metal on import, or a whole three.js scene, and each can react to sound, the
cursor, and even the tilt of a MacBook.

A wallpaper has no right to cost you anything, so it renders on the GPU, lives
in the menu bar, and pauses under fullscreen apps, in Low Power Mode, and while
the display sleeps.
