I built Atlas end to end for BluePlan, whose engineers need one paragraph
somewhere in three thousand pages of standards, manuals, and drawings. You ask
it the way you'd ask a colleague, in English or Romanian, and every claim in the
answer cites its page. Click a citation and the PDF opens beside the chat, at
that page.

People act on these answers, and an answer you can't check is worse than none,
so nothing comes back unsourced. It's been in daily production use since it
shipped.
