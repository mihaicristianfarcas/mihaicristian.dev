BluePlan, the studio I freelance with, designs architecture and structure
together, from the first sketch to the drawings a builder works from. So rather
than say that, the site shows it: a house that builds itself as you scroll, from
the column grid and the footings to the services in x-ray and a walk through
the finished rooms. It's all generated in three.js, with no model files, and
the page still scores 98 on mobile Lighthouse.

It's still a work in progress, ahead of its launch at blueplan.ro.
