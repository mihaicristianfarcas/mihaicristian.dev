Testing is when you notice things, and stopping to type them up is what breaks
the flow. With asides you say them instead: press <kbd>⌃⌥Space</kbd>, say what's
wrong, and keep testing. A few seconds later Whisper has transcribed it on your
Mac, word for word, and appended it to a Markdown note in the repo, with any
screenshot you copied right where you said it. There's no account, and no audio
leaves the machine.

The note is also where a coding agent picks it up. asides runs an MCP server,
so Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode can listen while you test and take each aside
as a prompt as soon as it's transcribed. A good part of this site's redesign was
asked for that way.
