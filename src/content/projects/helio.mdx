**Helio** is one app for your computers: the client you sit at and the host
you stream from, in the same window. It takes pingpong's place, with Ping and
Pong folded into one: your hosts on one side, this computer on the other, and a
switch that lets your other devices play it. macOS, Windows and Linux, as
client and host.

It aims at Moonlight-class streaming: HEVC or H.264, hardware encode and
decode, HDR and YUV 4:4:4, stereo to 7.1, controllers with rumble, and a
virtual display at the client's exact mode on Windows and macOS, and on Linux
under GNOME or KDE on Wayland, so nothing is scaled or letterboxed. Every
packet still travels inside [pq-boringtun](/projects/pq-boringtun/), the
post-quantum WireGuard from my thesis; pairing is hybrid SPAKE2 + ML-KEM.
Hosts are found through sealed records on the BitTorrent DHT, and NATs are
punched from both sides, so there's no relay server in between.

Agents can use your hosts as clients of their own, from Helio or any MCP
client, with the host enforcing what each device may do. The clipboard goes
both ways. Helio is still in the works: 1.0.0 is coming soon, and the full
benchmarks are on the way. A first loopback run puts capture to screen at
5.1 ms; the full write-up, with glass-to-glass figures, is still to come.
