*Ukiyo*, the floating world. I'd used editors every day for years without once
thinking about the machinery, and the only way I know to stop wondering about
something is to build it: modes, a file explorer, line wrapping, and undo. The
classic design patterns each found a place they genuinely fit. Modes are
States, every edit is a Command that knows how to undo itself, and the explorer
is a Composite. That taught me more than the editor did.
