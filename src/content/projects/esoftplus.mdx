Most landing pages say the product is polished, then ask you to book a demo.
This one shows you: as you scroll, the dashboard unfolds out of its oval, crops
down to the mobile app, and tooltips point out what each part does. There's no
animation library underneath, only scroll position mapped onto CSS custom
properties, which is why it stays smooth even when you throw the page.
