**Ping** is the client you sit at, **Pong** the host you stream from. They
replace my own Apollo and Moonlight setup with the one thing it couldn't give
me: every packet travels inside [pq-boringtun](/projects/pq-boringtun/), the
post-quantum WireGuard from my thesis. Written from scratch in Rust, on the same
two machines it shows more frames than Moonlight and Apollo: 115.6 a second to
their 110.

It reaches your host from anywhere with no relay server in between, and can
lend it to an AI agent that you watch, take over, or stop.
