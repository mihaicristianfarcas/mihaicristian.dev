Most note apps are filing cabinets: good at putting something away, and no
help while an idea is still shapeless. Adso is for that part. Blocks you drag
around, notes nested as deep as the thought goes, and a chat beside the page
that has read it, so you can ask what a sprawling note actually says.

It's named after the novice in *The Name of the Rose*, who spends the entire
book taking notes on something he doesn't understand yet. I related.
